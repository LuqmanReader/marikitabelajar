What is LedgerPeer?

LedgerPeer is a student-focused workspace built around FIA and ACCA revision, study planning and personal financial organisation.

Exam attempt review

Keep track of chapter sets, progress tests and mock exams. Organise your results and use your mistakes to decide which accounting topics to revisit.

Revision dashboard

Bring together your subject progress, exam debriefs and topic-level learning priorities. The revision workflow supports Foundations in Accountancy (FIA) and ACCA students.

Study planner & calendar

Plan revision sessions and exam preparation. Use the calendar and planner to organise study goals, and the notes library to strengthen weak syllabus topics.

Financial manager

Track personal cash flow, expenses, savings, investments and financial goals alongside your studies. Financial records are managed in your own student workspace.

One place for academic and financial progress

LedgerPeer is currently a student beta. Features inside the revision and financial dashboards require a student account. This public page describes the tools but does not expose any private student records.
