## Outstanding content acceptance criteria

Each of the 605 outstanding topics needs independent content development, not automated paraphrase. Required: (1) mapped syllabus learning outcomes and variant; (2) coherent explanations that teach definitions and decision logic; (3) accurate step-by-step workings or structured applied cases; (4) exam-standard practice with plausible distractors and reviewed solutions; (5) common errors, self-check and source/edition review; (6) consistent visual, table or diagram where it helps comprehension.

All new material is independently authored and not copied from proprietary Kaplan or ACCA books. Do not mark a chapter as an official ACCA textbook or a complete certified 2026–27 lesson. The ACCA Study Hub remains the official full-syllabus reference for eligible students.
