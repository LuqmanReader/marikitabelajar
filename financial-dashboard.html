Financial security & wealth drag

Your configurable targets

Emergency fund

Designated emergency cash ÷ your bare-minimum monthly expense baseline. Target: 3–6 months, configurable below.

Financial independence progress

Investment portfolio ÷ (annual expense baseline × target multiple). The default 25× is a planning assumption, not a guaranteed retirement outcome. Cash and your home are excluded.

Weighted fund expense ratio

—

Annual cost estimate = recorded lot value × annual fee %. Average is weighted across lots with known fees. Unknown fees are excluded, not treated as zero.

Retirement / tax account contributions

Contributions tagged in the tracker, up to the selected month. The target is your own annual goal; it is not a statutory cap or a tax-deduction calculation.
